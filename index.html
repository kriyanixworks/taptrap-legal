Privacy Policy

TapTrap
Developer: Kriyanix Works
Last updated: 7 November 2026

TapTrap is a mobile game developed by Kriyanix Works. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used when you play the game and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect

TapTrap does not require you to create an account or provide personal details to play.

The game saves information such as your level, coins, hearts, unlocked themes, game progress and settings on your device.

We do not directly collect your name, phone number or location through the game.

2. Advertising

TapTrap uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google may collect information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, app activity and diagnostic information to provide advertisements, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

Depending on your location and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy.

3. Feedback and Support

If you choose to send feedback through TapTrap, we may receive:

  • The type of issue you report
  • Your message or suggestion
  • Your email address, if you provide one

We use this information only to review feedback, fix problems and respond to you when needed.

You do not need to submit feedback to play TapTrap.

4. How Your Game Data Is Stored

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device.

Depending on your Android backup settings, some saved data may also be included in your device's Google backup.

TapTrap does not require an online account or upload your game progress to our own servers.

5. Sharing Information

We do not sell the personal information you submit through our feedback form.

Google may process information through AdMob and the services used to receive feedback, according to its own privacy policies.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can use Reset Game Progress in Settings to restart your progress. This does not delete your coins, unlocked themes or other saved settings.

Uninstalling TapTrap normally removes the game's locally stored data. Copies may remain in Android backups.

If you have submitted feedback, you can contact us to request the removal of that information.

7. Children's Privacy

TapTrap does not require children or other players to provide personal information to play.

Advertisements are provided through Google AdMob. We will apply the appropriate advertising and privacy settings based on the age groups and countries where the game is available.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its features change.

The latest version will be available through the Privacy Policy link in TapTrap.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions, feedback or requests relating to information you have submitted, please use the Feedback & Support option in TapTrap.
